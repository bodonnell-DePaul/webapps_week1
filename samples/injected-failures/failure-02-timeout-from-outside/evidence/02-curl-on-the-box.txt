================================================================================
D) the reverse proxy in front of the app
================================================================================

ubuntu@campuspulse-demo:~$ sudo systemctl is-active caddy
active

ubuntu@campuspulse-demo:~$ curl -sS --max-time 6 -o /dev/null \
    --resolve status.campuspulse-demo.example:443:127.0.0.1 \
    -w "%{http_code} in %{time_total}s\n" \
    https://status.campuspulse-demo.example/healthz
200 in 0.011s

  ^ note the technique. --resolve forces the connection to loopback while keeping
    the real hostname for SNI and certificate validation, so this test does NOT
    disable verification. Reaching for -k here would have destroyed the evidence
    that the certificate is fine, which is exactly the mistake Block A opened on.
